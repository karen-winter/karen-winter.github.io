Ingredient Lookup Tool

I try new products a lot. I used to look up every ingredient one by one. Now I paste the full ingredient list into my Google Sheet and get everything in one table.

  • Google Sheets
  • XLOOKUP
  • FILTER
  • REGEXREPLACE
  • LET / LAMBDA

How it works

  1. I paste a product's ingredient list into one cell.
  2. The sheet splits it into one row per ingredient, in the same order as the label. Order matters because ingredients near the top are used in larger amounts.
  3. Each ingredient is matched to its standard name using my mapping table, so different spellings on different labels still find the right ingredient.
  4. It pulls in the EWG score range and my flags for each ingredient, plus my notes on why I avoid it.
  5. It shows which products I've used before that have the same ingredient. That helps me guess if a new product might break me out.

What it flags

The top of the sheet gives a quick summary of the product:

  • Total number of ingredients
  • High-risk ingredients (EWG 8 or higher)
  • Ingredients on my avoid list
  • Carcinogen, cancer, hormone, acne, allergy and irritation, and contamination concerns
  • New ingredients that aren't in my list yet, so I know what to research

Two more search tabs

I also built a product search and an ingredient search. I can type part of a product name or an ingredient and see every match with its scores and flags.

What I learned building it

My first formulas worked on the raw ingredient text. They counted things like "may contain" as an ingredient. I rebuilt them to use the product_ingredient table, and the counts came out right.

I built the formulas with help from Gemini. I worked through each one until I understood what it was doing, and kept notes on every change in a formula tab.

The sheet itself stays private because it holds the data I collected by hand. The screenshots show how it works.
